Loops. seam is the wrap discontinuity: the sample jump across the wrap minus the 99.9th-percentile jump the waveform already makes on its own, so "clean" means no click is physically present. head/tail is the new check — the RMS of the last 200 ms minus the first 200 ms. Round 1 shipped ambient_downtempo_take1 at +7.8 dB there: click-free, but it would audibly drop in level on every wrap. Round 2 searches the fold point (fold = crossfade seconds / tail cut seconds) until the delta is under 3 dB, trying the round-1 fold first so loops that were already fine are untouched.
