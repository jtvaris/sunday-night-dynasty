Read this before trusting the order. Nobody in the generation loop could hear these files. The # rank is derived only from measurable proxies for the brief: peak @ is where the loudest moment falls (0 = opens loudest, 1 = ends loudest), build is the last third minus the first third in dB, arc is the spread between the quiet and loud sections, cent is the median spectral centroid (low = dark and bass-led, high = bright and thin) and LRA is loudness range. A theme that "builds to a peak" should show a late peak, a positive build and a wide arc. None of that can tell you whether the melody is any good, whether the brass sounds synthetic, or whether the model hallucinated wordless vocal pads — that judgement is yours, by ear.
