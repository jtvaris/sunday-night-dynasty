# Privacy Policy

**App:** Sunday Night Dynasty
**Developer:** Juha Varis
**Contact:** juha.t.varis@gmail.com
**Effective date:** 10 August 2026
**Last updated:** 10 August 2026

## Summary

Sunday Night Dynasty does not collect any data.

The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no
server of its own. It does not make network requests. Everything you do in the game stays
on your device.

## Data we collect

**None.** We do not collect, receive, transmit, sell, or share any personal data or usage
data. Because no data ever leaves your device, there is nothing for us to store, access,
or disclose.

This corresponds to the **"Data Not Collected"** declaration on the app's App Store
privacy label.

## Data stored on your device

The app saves data locally so the game works between sessions. This data stays in the
app's own storage container on your device and is never transmitted anywhere:

- **Save games and career progress** — leagues, teams, rosters, schedules, results and
  any other game state you create while playing.
- **App settings and preferences** — options you choose in the app's Settings screen.
- **Crash and performance diagnostics** — the app uses Apple's on-device **MetricKit**
  framework to write crash, hang and CPU-exception reports to a private folder inside the
  app's container. These files remain on your device. They are **not uploaded**, not sent
  to us, and not shared with anyone. Only the newest reports are kept; older ones are
  deleted automatically.

Deleting the app removes all of this data from your device. This local data may also be
included in your own device backups (iCloud Backup or an encrypted computer backup) if you
have backups enabled — that is a feature of iOS, controlled by you in the Settings app, and
we have no access to those backups.

## Third parties

The app contains no third-party SDKs, no analytics libraries, no advertising networks, and
no social-media integrations. No third party receives any information about you through
this app.

## Apple

If you download or purchase the app, Apple handles that transaction under Apple's own
privacy policy. We receive only anonymous, aggregated sales and download statistics from
Apple through App Store Connect. Those reports contain no information that identifies you.

Should you choose to send Apple diagnostics and usage data (Settings → Privacy & Security →
Analytics & Improvements), Apple may share crash reports with us as the developer. That
sharing is controlled entirely by you and by Apple, not by this app.

## Children

The app is safe for general audiences and, since it collects no data at all, it collects no
data from children. It contains no advertising, no in-app purchases, no user-generated
content, no chat, and no links out to the web.

## Your rights

Data-protection laws such as the EU/EEA GDPR and the California CCPA give you rights to
access, correct, delete and port your personal data, and to object to its processing.
Because we hold no personal data about you whatsoever, there is nothing for us to retrieve
or erase on request. You remain in full control of the data on your own device and can
remove all of it at any time by deleting the app.

## Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app changes what it does with data, this policy will be updated
before that version is released, and the "Last updated" date above will change. The current
version of this policy always applies to the current version of the app.
